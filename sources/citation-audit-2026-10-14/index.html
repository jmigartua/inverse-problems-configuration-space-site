C01Needs qualification or a better citation

Hadamard and the modern definition

Website claim

Hadamard 1902 supplies existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence; an underdetermined inverse problem fails the second and third.

Whereecosystem/data/sources.yml:70
Evidence and assessment

The original scan opens on printed p. 49 by explaining bien posé in terms of possibility and determinacy. It is an appropriate historical reference, but the modern three-part formulation needs a precise modern source. Non-uniqueness alone does not establish a separate instability result for a chosen regularized solution.

  • Printed pp. 49–50; opening definition and Cauchy/Dirichlet discussionhadamard1902recordPDF p. 1, 2full text
Proposed wording or action

Hadamard provides a historical discussion of well-posedness. In the modern formulation, existence, uniqueness and continuous dependence are separate requirements; non-uniqueness violates uniqueness. Add a modern definition with its spaces and topology.

C07Needs qualification or a better citation

Homometry versus a linear null space

Website claim

Homometric structures give identical intensities and constitute an exact null space.

Whereecosystem/data/sources.yml:59ecosystem/site/cases/rietveld/index.qmd:20ecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:20
Evidence and assessment

Patterson’s publisher abstract supports distinct non-congruent point sets with the same vector-distance set. It supports non-uniqueness, not the stronger characterization as a linear null space or necessarily a continuous family. For weighted atomic species the relevant equality is the weighted autocorrelation, not an unweighted set of vectors alone.

  • Publisher abstractpatterson1944recorddoi 10.1103/PhysRev.65.195abstract only
Independent reasoning

A nonlinear map can have disconnected preimages without a positive-dimensional fibre. For \(F(x)=x^2\), \(F^{-1}(1)=\{-1,+1\}\), while \(F'(\pm1)\) is nonzero.

Proposed wording or action

Homometric structures provide distinct solutions with the same diffraction intensities. Describe these as exact ambiguities; reserve “null space” for a linear map or its local Jacobian.

C11Needs qualification or a better citation

ISODISPLACE and orthonormal basis conventions

Website claim

The mode decomposition is presented generically as an orthonormal change of basis, with the same number of independent coordinates.

Whereecosystem/data/sources.yml:43ecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:40
Evidence and assessment

Campbell et al. support an invertible change of basis within the chosen distortion space and equal independent degrees of freedom. They also note that their CLIROP modes need not be mutually orthogonal in non-rectangular cells. AMPLIMODES adopts an orthonormal convention. Neither statement means that selecting a subgroup or freezing modes preserves the original unrestricted space.

  • Printed pp. 610 and 613; CLIROP caveat and basis transformationcampbell2006recorddoi 10.1107/S0021889806014075PDF p. 5, 8full text
  • Printed pp. 823–824, basis normalizationorobengoa2009recorddoi 10.1107/S0021889809028064PDF p. 4, 5full text
Proposed wording or action

For a fixed parent, cell, subgroup and complete set of allowed modes, the mode amplitudes are alternative coordinates for the same allowed displacements. Specify normalization and orthogonalization; imposing additional symmetry or fixing modes restricts the model.

C12Needs qualification or a better citation

Primary-mode hierarchy and sparse priors

Website claim

One or two primary modes dominate; such prior knowledge cannot be written in atomic coordinates.

Whereecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:42ecosystem/site/cases/rietveld/index.qmd:27
Evidence and assessment

Perez-Mato et al. §4 explains energetic hierarchies, induced secondary modes and coupling-dependent amplitudes. This supports using a hierarchical prior in suitable displacive systems. It is not a universal one-or-two-mode theorem. An invertible basis change permits the same prior to be expressed in atomic coordinates, though less simply.

  • Printed pp. 564–565, §4, especially Eq. (9)perezmato2010recorddoi 10.1107/S0108767310016247PDF p. 7, 8full text
Proposed wording or action

For many pseudosymmetric displacive structures, a small subset of distortions dominates. Mode coordinates can make a physically motivated hierarchical prior easier to express and apply; the equivalent prior can also be transformed into atomic coordinates.

C13Incorrect as written

Symmetry modes do not necessarily diagonalize the data sensitivity

Website claim

Switching to symmetry modes turns ambiguous correlated coordinates into well-determined single amplitudes and aligns the Jacobian near-null space.

Whereecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:43ecosystem/site/cases/rietveld/index.qmd:27
Evidence and assessment

The papers support useful physical organization and sometimes reduced correlations. They do not establish that symmetry modes are the singular vectors of a particular instrument-weighted diffraction Jacobian. Perez-Mato’s discussion of privileged eigenmodes concerns the energy Hessian, not the measurement Fisher matrix.

  • Printed pp. 564–565, energy eigenmodesperezmato2010recorddoi 10.1107/S0108767310016247PDF p. 7, 8full text
  • Printed 010901-4, parameterization dependencetranstrum2015recorddoi 10.1063/1.4923066PDF p. 5full text
Independent reasoning

If \(u=Ba\) with B invertible, \(J_a=J_uB\) and \(\operatorname{rank}(J_a)=\operatorname{rank}(J_u)\). If B is orthogonal, J_a and J_u have the same singular values. Symmetry alone does not force B to diagonalize \(J_u^{\mathsf T}J_u\).

Proposed wording or action

Test how well the symmetry basis aligns with sensitive and insensitive directions for the chosen experiment. A complete invertible reparameterization preserves exact identifiability; improvement in inference requires additional constraints, priors, data, or a numerical benefit.

C14Incorrect as written

Amplitude magnitude is not uncertainty

Website claim

Large amplitudes are determined and small amplitudes are undetermined.

Whereecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:44ecosystem/site/cases/rietveld/index.qmd:27
Evidence and assessment

The mode papers rank the contribution of distortions to structure and stability. They do not equate amplitude size with statistical precision. That requires uncertainty or sensitivity analysis for the actual data.

  • §4 amplitude hierarchyperezmato2010recorddoi 10.1107/S0108767310016247PDF p. 7, 8full text
  • Printed p. 1873, Fig. 1 and sensitivity directionsgutenkunst2007recorddoi 10.1371/journal.pcbi.0030189PDF p. 3full text
Independent reasoning

In \(d=A+\varepsilon\) with known noise variance \(\sigma^2\), the local information about A is \(1/\sigma^2\), independent of whether A is zero, small, or large.

Proposed wording or action

Report mode amplitudes together with their uncertainties and correlations. Rank structural contribution and statistical identifiability separately.

C16Incorrect as written

A small singular value needs a parameter scale

Website claim

An unscaled singular value much smaller than one is a practical non-identifiability.

Whereecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:35ecosystem/site/programme/index.qmd:17
Evidence and assessment

Transtrum et al. explicitly warn that Fisher eigenvalues change under parameter units and reparameterization. Noise whitening alone does not make differently dimensioned parameter directions comparable.

  • Printed 010901-4, §III, unit-change exampletranstrum2015recorddoi 10.1063/1.4923066PDF p. 5full text

C17Incorrect as written

Local Jacobian rank is not a complete structural-identifiability test

Website claim

Zero Jacobian singular values diagnose structural non-identifiability, and structural non-identifiability is always a positive-dimensional fibre.

Whereecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:35ecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:87
Evidence and assessment

The website conflates local first-order rank deficiency, continuous non-identifiability, and global discrete ambiguities. Raue’s abstract supports the general distinction but not these equivalences. Exact elementary counterexamples settle the issue.

  • Abstract supports only the broader terminologyraue2009recorddoi 10.1093/bioinformatics/btp358abstract only
  • Abstract gives multiple non-congruent homometric configurationspatterson1944recorddoi 10.1103/PhysRev.65.195abstract only
Independent reasoning

\(F(x)=x^3\) is injective even though \(F'(0)=0\). \(F(x)=x^2\) has two preimages for positive data but nonzero derivative at each preimage. Thus neither stated equivalence holds without qualifications.

Proposed wording or action

Distinguish global from local identifiability, continuous from discrete ambiguities, and first-order rank deficiency from a structural proof. Use the Jacobian as a local diagnostic with regularity assumptions.

C18Incorrect as written

A likelihood region is not a posterior

Website claim

The chi-square tube is a posterior distribution.

Whereecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:77ecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:32
Evidence and assessment

Tarantola Eq. (1.100) includes a prior multiplying a likelihood. The website’s tube is a thresholded residual set, with no probability density or prior weighting. It is not itself a posterior or automatically a credible region.

  • Printed p. 35, Eqs. (1.94) and (1.100)tarantola2005recorddoi 10.1137/1.9780898717921PDF p. 49full text
  • Printed pp. 152–153, Eqs. (19)–(23)daun2019recorddoi 10.1016/j.jqsrt.2019.01.021PDF p. 7, 8full text
Proposed wording or action

The tube is a data-misfit acceptance region. A Bayesian posterior weights configurations using both likelihood and prior; a credible region is constructed from that normalized posterior.

C23Needs qualification or a better citation

Szymanski probabilities are phase-classification confidence

Website claim

Szymanski returns distributions instead of labels, providing the required form of output for an underdetermined inverse problem.

Whereecosystem/data/sources.yml:171ecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:61
Evidence and assessment

The paper uses 1,000 stochastic dropout predictions and defines a phase’s probability as its fraction of predicted labels. It ranks phases and uses a branching procedure to select a mixture. These are phase-classification confidence scores, not a posterior over continuous atomic or mode parameters, and the final result still identifies phases.

  • Publisher-typeset early-access PDF pp. 3–4 (C–D), CNN and branching sectionsszymanski2021recorddoi 10.1021/acs.chemmater.1c01071PDF p. 3, 4full text
Proposed wording or action

Szymanski combines physics-based augmentation with dropout-based phase confidence and a branching mixture-identification algorithm. Cite it as a related classification workflow, not as evidence of posterior inference over the refinement configuration space.

C25Needs qualification or a better citation

Kuzmenko’s background model and dense variational mesh

Website claim

Kuzmenko’s variational dielectric function is anchored by a few oscillators.

Whereecosystem/data/sources.yml:182ecosystem/site/cases/dielectric/index.qmd:25
Evidence and assessment

The central method independently adjusts the imaginary dielectric function on a dense anchor-frequency mesh and couples the real part by KK. The number of variational degrees of freedom can be comparable to the number of data points. A limited-parameter background model supplies extrapolation behaviour in a separate first fitting step. The phrase could refer to that background model, but it is ambiguous without this distinction; it should not stand alone as a description of the variational representation.

  • Printed 083108-1, -3 and -4; abstract and §IV, Eqs. (14) and (17)kuzmenko2005recorddoi 10.1063/1.1979470PDF p. 3, 5, 6full text
Proposed wording or action

Kuzmenko represents the dielectric function on a dense frequency mesh constrained by KK, with a background model for out-of-range spectral weight. Do not conflate this mesh with a small set of physical oscillators.

C26Needs qualification or a better citation

Phase recovery needs analytic and geometry conditions

Website claim

Causality alone fixes reflection phase from its modulus over the whole frequency axis.

Whereecosystem/site/cases/dielectric/index.qmd:25ecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:52
Evidence and assessment

Kuzmenko explicitly states the extra condition that r has no zeros in the upper complex-frequency half-plane for the logarithmic reflectivity relation. He notes failures for sufficiently oblique incidence and thin films. The standard relation couples phase to log modulus, not directly to modulus.

  • Printed 083108-1, Eqs. (3)–(4) and adjacent caveatkuzmenko2005recorddoi 10.1063/1.1979470PDF p. 3full text
Proposed wording or action

For geometries satisfying the analytic conditions for log r, such as the standard semi-infinite normal-incidence case, KK relates reflection phase to log modulus, with extrapolation assumptions outside the measured range.

C27Incorrect as written

Whereecosystem/site/cases/dielectric/index.qmd:19
Evidence and assessment

Kuzmenko §IV.D explicitly explains that high- and low-frequency spectral weight contributes to the real dielectric function inside the measured band. Extrapolation uncertainty is real, but it is not an arbitrary exact null space of all out-of-band changes.

  • Printed 083108-4, §IV.Dkuzmenko2005recorddoi 10.1063/1.1979470PDF p. 6full text
Proposed wording or action

Out-of-band spectral weight can affect in-band optical response through KK coupling. Finite-band data may constrain it weakly and ambiguously, so extrapolation assumptions and additional measurements remain important.

C28Incorrect as written

Oscillator non-identifiability is not universal

Website claim

A finite oscillator decomposition is necessarily non-unique even if the dielectric function is known.

Whereecosystem/site/cases/dielectric/index.qmd:19ecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:52
Evidence and assessment

Kuzmenko’s flexible variational representation is not a theorem that every fixed finite Lorentz model is structurally non-identifiable. Overlap, finite bandwidth, unknown model order and noise can make fits weakly determined. Exact degeneracies require separate conditions.

  • Printed 083108-3–4, variational representationkuzmenko2005recorddoi 10.1063/1.1979470PDF p. 5, 6full text
Independent reasoning

A rational response with fixed distinct simple poles has a unique partial-fraction representation up to ordering. Coincident poles can instead make individual strengths degenerate. These are different cases.

Proposed wording or action

Different oscillator models may fit finite-band noisy data comparably well. Assess identifiability for the specified model order and parameterization; do not assert universal exact non-uniqueness.

C35Incorrect as written

C36Needs qualification or a better citation

Low-frequency Drude degeneracy is a limiting approximation

Website claim

At low frequency only \(\omega_p^2\tau\) enters the measured spectrum, creating exact non-identifiability of the full model.

Whereecosystem/site/discourse/index.qmd:20ecosystem/site/cases/drude/index.qmd:36
Evidence and assessment

Daun Eq. (4) retains separate parameter dependence at finite frequency. The product controls DC conductivity and the leading dissipative low-frequency term. Treating a finite-band full Drude/Mie measurement as exactly product-only requires an explicit reduced forward model and limit.

  • Printed p. 149, Eqs. (4)–(5)daun2019recorddoi 10.1016/j.jqsrt.2019.01.021PDF p. 4full text
Independent reasoning

For \(\omega\tau\ll 1\), \(\varepsilon_I=1-\omega_p^2\tau^2+O(\omega^2)\) while \(\varepsilon_{II}=\omega_p^2\tau/\omega+O(\omega)\). The subleading information can be small, but the full expression is not solely a function of the product.

Proposed wording or action

In a reduced DC/leading-order model the data may depend only on \(\omega_p^2\tau\). In the full finite-frequency optical model, assess whether the separate sensitivities are merely too weak to resolve.

C41Incorrect as written

A covariance diagonal is not a conditional tube cross-section

Evidence and assessment

In the local Gaussian approximation, diagonal covariance entries are marginal variances along parameter axes and can already be large because of sloppy directions. Reading only the diagonal loses orientation and dependence, not all contribution from long directions. Nonlinearity can invalidate the approximation.

Proposed wording or action

Report covariance eigenvectors and correlations as well as marginal standard errors; use nonlinear exploration where the local ellipsoid is inadequate.

On this page

    What this is

    The working site of a line of thought: inverse problems whose theory is known but whose experiment collapses the configuration space, so that the parameters cannot be read back from the measurement without a tool brought from outside — a change of coordinates, a prior, another experiment, knowledge written into scripts and learned over at scale. The ideas are Iñigo González de Arrieta's; this is their development, with three cases, a programme, the sources and a dated log.
