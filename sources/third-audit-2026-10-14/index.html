Scope and method
The preceding conversation review reread the original 42 claim records, compared their website locations, and rechecked selected critical source passages. It also accessed Raue's full online article, whose definitions had previously been assessed only through the abstract. The seven findings below preserve that review's substantive conclusions, with stable identifiers R01–R07 and links added for this public edition. They are seven methodological and substantive issues, not a new classification of all 42 claims. The mathematical cautions and additional gaps are reviewer reasoning unless a source is explicitly identified.

It does not claim to have independently repeated every check in L012. In particular, the second report accepts C34 as a factual error and summarizes C25–C27 together; this report distinguishes ambiguous wording from a contradicted factual assertion. All three records remain proposals for human review.

Assessment

C24 paraphrases a comparison with three papers as a claim that the wider field does not perform the proposed workflow. Those are different assertions. Broader novelty claims occur elsewhere on the website, but require their own exact quotations and locations. The second report also recognizes this distinction in C24. In C34, the audit inserts “peak” into its paraphrase. The measurement begins at 205 nm, so it excludes the peak near 190 nm but captures part of the resonance feature. Clarification is useful; ambiguous wording is a fairer assessment than an unequivocal factual error in the original sentence.

The website sentences and Daun's measured range and explanation of the SPR feature.

R03New evidence to incorporate

The full Raue article exposes a consequential difference in definitions

Audit findingC06C17C39
Assessment

Raue's full article was accessible during the conversation review. In §2.3.2, its operational definition of practical non-identifiability requires an unbounded likelihood-based confidence region despite a unique likelihood minimum. That is more specific than a parameter being poorly constrained or having a small sensitivity. C06 correctly identifies the paper's subject, but this does not establish that the website uses its definitions faithfully. The earlier abstract-only limitation was honestly recorded; the newly accessible full text allows that evidence gap to be narrowed.

Checked against

Full online article, §§2.3.1–2.3.2, Definition 1, and §3. Selected sections were read; this is not a claim to have read the entire article in full.

  • §§2.3.2 and 3, practical non-identifiability and limits of curvature-based diagnosticsraue2009recorddoi 10.1093/bioinformatics/btp358full text
Proposed wording or action

Qualify C06 by stating the definition actually used. Distinguish local sensitivity, broad practical imprecision, and Raue's particular criterion. Preserve the earlier access status as historical and record this evidence update separately; do not promote the source to read in full.

R04State the assumptions

The mathematical corrections need explicit assumptions and limits

Audit findingC13C17C28C41
Independent reasoning

C13 correctly rejects information creation through an invertible change of coordinates. Mode coordinates can nevertheless make identifiable combinations easier to interpret and computation easier to perform. C17's counterexamples distinguish local Jacobian rank from global uniqueness, but discrete global ambiguity and continuous local non-identifiability need separate names; literature conventions must be stated. C41's inverse-information covariance argument requires nonsingular information and the relevant local likelihood approximation. A prior changes posterior curvature, and a singular matrix cannot simply be inverted. C28's uniqueness of a partial-fraction representation does not by itself prove identifiability of every physical oscillator parameterization: it challenges universal non-uniqueness without supplying a complete general proof.

Assessment

These qualifications preserve the core mathematical criticisms. They prevent a corrective report from replacing one unconditional assertion with another or from dismissing useful computational and interpretive benefits.

Proposed wording or action

Attach the assumptions to each formula or counterexample. State precisely which assertion is disproved, and distinguish that result from a full identifiability proof for a specified physical model.

R06Extend the coverage

Important claims remain outside the original audit's direct assessments

WhereDiscourse: dielectric exampleDiscourse: learningDiscourse: definitions
Independent reasoning

The following are additional issues identified by this reviewer, not new results attributed to the cited papers.

  • The statement that only a distribution is honest and every point estimate should be rejected is too strong. A point estimate can be useful when its assumptions, uncertainty and decision purpose are explicit; non-identifiability does not impose one mandatory reporting format.
  • The requirement that mode parameters vary smoothly with temperature is a conditional physical assumption. A discontinuity can reflect a real transition.
  • Repeated structured residuals can arise from shared calibration errors, instrument effects or preprocessing; they do not uniquely diagnose a wrong physical model.
  • Published databases reflect selection, measurement and publication practices as well as the configurations observed in nature. They are not automatically a representative distribution of nature's configurations.
  • A large ratio between sensitivity eigenvalues does not alone establish inadequate absolute precision. Sloppiness and practical identifiability require separate assessment with stated scales and noise.
Assessment

Coverage of all 21 source entries is not equivalent to checking every associated scientific assertion. The first audit was explicit that it was targeted, but its broad coverage statement can still be overread. The second report identifies further gaps of its own; neither gap list should be treated as exhaustive.

Proposed wording or action

Give these assertions their own exact quotations, locations and assessments in a future revision. Do not infer that unlisted statements have passed review.

R07Revise the reporting method

Verdict totals are bookkeeping, not a quality score

Assessment

The original totals are internally consistent, but the categories mix different dimensions: truth, citation support, access limitations and research proposals. Several findings also concern the same underlying passage. Counts of claim groups are neither independent observations nor an error rate for the website. The second report's acceptance totals likewise record that reviewer's judgments, not human approval to apply changes.

Proposed wording or action

For each assessed assertion, record the exact website quotation, source evidence, reviewer reasoning, assessment, confidence and proposed action separately. Distinguish contradicted, unsupported, ambiguous and dependent on assumptions. Keep access status separate from scientific correctness and an implementation proposal separate from an accepted decision.

Findings that remain valuable

The strongest findings survive this review: Daun discounts surface scattering for the particles considered in that case (C33); a thresholded misfit set is not a posterior distribution (C18); amplitude magnitude is not uncertainty (C14); and an invertible coordinate change cannot manufacture experimental information (C13). These are reasons to retain the audit while making its own claims equally precise.

On this page

    What this is

    The working site of a line of thought: inverse problems whose theory is known but whose experiment collapses the configuration space, so that the parameters cannot be read back from the measurement without a tool brought from outside — a change of coordinates, a prior, another experiment, knowledge written into scripts and learned over at scale. The ideas are Iñigo González de Arrieta's; this is their development, with three cases, a programme, the sources and a dated log.
